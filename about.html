About Me

I'm Reuben Lotsima, a creative and full-stack digital marketer based in Joburg. I build content that doesn't just look good but also moves people. The kind that makes you pause mid-scroll, double back, and actually remember the brand behind it.

I call myself the Content King not because of ego, but because it's a name I'm intentionally building and a mindset I live by. I understand one simple truth: relevance is royalty. In a world where attention is the real currency, those who understand relevance rule.

I operate at the intersection of creativity, strategy, and culture. Video, design, social media, and storytelling, all backed by intention. I don't create for algorithms. I create for people. I study how audiences scroll, where they pause, what they ignore, and what makes them stop in their tracks. That understanding guides every creative decision I make.

As a Gen Z creative who is deeply plugged into online culture, I understand trends, humour, attention spans, and how quickly audiences spot inauthenticity. Creativity without strategy is decoration. Strategy without creativity is forgettable. I aim for the space where both collide and actually move the needle.

How It Started

My journey into content didn't start in a fancy studio or agency boardroom. It started in a church. Managing my church's social media showed me the real power of content when it connects emotionally and drives action. People didn't just watch, they showed up, they engaged and they were moved. That curiosity turned into obsession.

Before fully stepping into the creative world, I worked in retail at Hamleys. Selling toys might sound unrelated, but it taught me something priceless: sales. How to grab attention fast, how people behave when they're curious, distracted, or excited, and what makes them stop in their tracks.

Those experiences shaped how I think today. Content is psychology before it is pixels.

I'm a recent graduate of iHub Africa's 2025 cohort, a 10-month digital skills and employability programme focused on developing industry-ready digital creatives. Alongside technical training in digital marketing and content creation, the programme placed a strong emphasis on soft skills, professionalism, and personal growth, shaping how I work with people as much as how I create.
